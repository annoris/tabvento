Tabvento uses a prospective source-available commercial license for new
Tabvento-owned contributions, with a free Simulated Demo Mode and paid official
Live Browser Mode activation. The operative permissions are in [LICENSE](../LICENSE).
This document explains release boundaries and the remaining legal review; it
is not a representation that any particular claim will be enforceable.

Previously promised permanent activation for a local data directory remains

payment. They do not promise support or all future releases.

## Rights that remain available

The new license covers only new copyrightable contributions owned by Tabvento
and first published under it. Earlier MIT grants continue to apply to material
already distributed under those grants, even when that material appears in a
newer checkout. A new root license cannot recover previously granted freedoms.

The bundled Browser Use engine, dependencies, and fonts retain their independent
licenses. [Third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) preserve the upstream
MIT grant and identify the SIL OFL font licenses. The commercial activation
restriction must not be described as overriding these rights, preventing
independent use of the upstream engine, or charging for rights already granted.

Public GitHub viewing and forking remain permitted by
[GitHub's terms, section D.5](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-of-service).
Making a repository private later does not erase existing copies or forks;
see [GitHub's licensing guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/licensing-a-repository).

## Florida and United States legal review before commercial rollout

A Florida-licensed attorney should review the license, actual seller identity,
chain of title, historical grants, payment presentation, and customer process
before the revised commercial terms are relied upon. The licensor is Annoris
Fernandez, operating the Tabvento project in Florida, USA. "Tabvento" is the
project name; these documents do not assert that it is a separate corporation
or supply an unverified business address.

The attorney review must resolve:

- Which new contributions Tabvento owns and can license, with a release/version
  boundary and preserved upstream attribution. Shared or previously licensed
  code cannot simply be classified as exclusively proprietary.
- The seller's contact details, consumer disclosures, delivery and
  activation commitments, and refund/cancellation remedies required by law.
  This license does not impose an automatic no-refund policy.
- A clear payment offer and affirmative acceptance of the exact terms before
  payment, with a retainable copy and proportionate evidence of assent. A
  transaction signature proves a blockchain transaction; it does not by itself
  prove the payer's identity or agreement to a contract.
- The distinction between permitted source access, payment-contract claims,
  and unauthorized exercise of copyright permissions. Do not send demands or
  takedowns merely because someone downloaded or forked a public repository.

Florida's [Uniform Electronic Transaction Act, section 668.50](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/668.50)
recognizes electronic records and signatures, while the existence and effect of
agreement depend on the transaction and other applicable law. It does not turn
an unnoticed LICENSE file into automatic agreement. Florida
[section 501.204](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/0501.204)
prohibits unfair and deceptive commercial practices; payment and activation
claims must accurately match the delivered product.

United States [17 USC 106](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-120/t17/s106/)
identifies exclusive copyright rights subject to statutory limitations, including
[17 USC 117](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title17/html/USCODE-2024-title17-chap1-sec117.htm)
for computer programs. Whether conduct is infringement, contractual breach, or
permitted use requires analysis of the actual rights, facts, and applicable law.
GitHub's [DMCA guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/guide-to-submitting-a-dmca-takedown-notice)
requires identifying protected work and considering the recipient's license;
a takedown is not a payment-collection tool.

## Implementation status

This licensing change does not implement a checkout, acceptance record,
identity check, or remote entitlement service. The existing on-chain activation
gate remains a local technical control. Documentation alone does not establish
contract formation, prove worldwide enforceability, or make a local gate
impossible to modify. Commercial launch review must assess the actual product
flow as well as the written terms.
